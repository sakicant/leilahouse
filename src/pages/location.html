Local highlights

What's around House Leila

Nearest beach

House Leila is a short 100 metre walk from the beach in Zablaće, so you can enjoy the sea without ever starting your car. For a premium resort experience, the beaches of Amadria Park Resort are only a few minutes away.

Local market & restaurants

A local market, a bakery and several restaurants sit within a 150 metre walk in the centre of Zablaće. Have your morning coffee by the sea or a traditional seafood dinner at a konoba, without driving into the city.

Šibenik city centre

The historic centre is a 7 km drive away. Spend the afternoon exploring the UNESCO-listed St. James Cathedral, dining on the waterfront, and wandering the ancient stone streets of one of the oldest native Croatian towns on the Adriatic.

Aquapark Dalmatia

Families are a quick drive from Aquapark Dalmatia, the first themed water park in Croatia, inside the Amadria Park complex. Premium resort entertainment by day, the quiet privacy of your own house by night.

Krka National Park

House Leila is a great base for Krka National Park, with its waterfalls, walking trails and unforgettable scenery. On the way you can stop in Skradin, the charming riverside gateway town to the park.

Split & Zadar airports

Zablaće sits in the heart of Dalmatia, halfway between Split and Zadar airports. Whichever you fly into, the house is less than an hour's drive away.

Day trips

Waterfalls, islands and old stone towns

With House Leila as your base you are perfectly positioned for easy day trips to the spectacular waterfalls of Krka National Park. You can also catch a short boat ride to the nearby islands of Zlarin or Prvić and spend the day hopping between quiet, car-free Mediterranean fishing villages.

Because you are exactly halfway between Split and Zadar, the lively coastal towns of Vodice and Primošten are a quick drive in either direction.
