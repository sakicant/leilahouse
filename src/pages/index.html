Where you'll be

The best of the Adriatic coast, at your doorstep

House Leila sits 100 metres from the nearest beach and only minutes from the Amadria Park hotels, their beaches and Aquapark Dalmatia — luxury resort amenities without the resort price tag.

Enjoy the calm of a quiet seaside village while being a 7 km drive from the historic centre of Šibenik, and use the house as a base camp for the waterfalls of Krka National Park and the riverside town of Skradin.
