Where you'll be

Where you actually are

Zablaće is a working village on a small peninsula, not a resort strip. The Amadria Park beaches and Aquapark Dalmatia are close enough for a day out when the children need one, and far enough that you do not hear them.

Šibenik and its cathedral are 7 km away. Krka National Park and Skradin make an easy day trip, and the boats to the car-free islands of Zlarin and Prvić leave from the Šibenik waterfront.

Split and Zadar airports are both under an hour away, which is worth knowing before you book flights.
