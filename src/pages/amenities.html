The essentials

The things that matter most

Air-conditioned comfort

In all three bedrooms and the living room. In July and August you will use it, and it is included in the rate rather than metered.

Fast & free Wi-Fi

Throughout the house and out on the terrace. Fast enough to stream in the evening, or work a morning if you have to.

Secure private parking

One space inside the fence, free, no permit needed. Free public parking 50 metres away if you arrive in two cars.

Modern kitchen

Dishwasher, oven, hob, coffee maker, ice machine, and a spice rack that is actually stocked. You can cook properly here, not just reheat.

Garden & stone BBQ

Fenced and private, with a stone BBQ, a long table under the pergola, and an outdoor shower by the door.

Laundry for longer stays

A washing machine and the powder to go with it, which matters more than people expect on a two week stay with children.
