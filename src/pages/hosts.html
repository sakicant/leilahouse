Our story

Why we still have the house

We are Hrvojka and Tomislav, both from the Šibenik area. Zablaće is the sort of village where the beach is never full and the bakery knows what you want by the third morning. That is most of the reason we rent the house out rather than sell it.

We fitted it out the way we would want a house to be if we were the ones arriving after a long drive. Everything works, nothing is too precious to use, and six people are not living on top of each other.
