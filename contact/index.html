Before you write

Quick booking questions

How quickly do you reply?
We monitor inquiries daily and aim to answer all emails and WhatsApp messages within a few hours.
Can I book directly?
Yes. When you contact us directly through this page you avoid the service fees charged by the major booking platforms, and you get the lowest available rate.
What are the check-in and check-out times?
Standard check-in is from 15:00 and check-out is by 10:00. If you need special arrangements for your arrival in Zablaće, mention it in your message and we will do our best to accommodate your schedule.
Can you help with an airport transfer?
Yes. If you have already booked and need help getting to House Leila, we can arrange your transfer with our family partner, TAXI Antonio.
