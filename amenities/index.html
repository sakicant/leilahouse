Amenities

Amenities & features

The comfort of a modern hotel with the privacy of your own house — everything you need for a stress-free Dalmatian holiday.

We have designed every detail of House Leila for a comfortable coastal getaway. From a fully stocked kitchen to the Mediterranean garden outside, the house is set up for a quick weekend or a full two-week summer holiday.

The essentials

What guests notice first

Air-conditioned comfort

Air conditioning in every bedroom and in the living room, so the house stays comfortable through the hottest Dalmatian afternoons.

Fast & free Wi-Fi

Free Wi-Fi throughout the house — for planning day trips around Šibenik, sharing beach photos, or relaxing indoors after the beach.

Secure private parking

Free private fenced parking on-site, plus additional free public parking 50 m away if you arrive with a second car.

Modern kitchen

A fully equipped kitchen with dishwasher, oven, coffee maker and a full set of spices — everything for comfortable self-catering.

Garden & stone BBQ

Your own Mediterranean garden with a shaded terrace, traditional stone BBQ and an outdoor shower for after the beach.

Laundry for longer stays

A private washing machine and laundry essentials — a real difference on longer family holidays and beach vacations.
