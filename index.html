Where you'll be

The best of the Adriatic coast, at your doorstep

House Leila sits 100 metres from the nearest beach and only minutes from the Amadria Park hotels, their beaches and Aquapark Dalmatia — luxury resort amenities without the resort price tag.

Enjoy the calm of a quiet seaside village while being a 7 km drive from the historic centre of Šibenik, and use the house as a base camp for the waterfalls of Krka National Park and the riverside town of Skradin.

Good to know

Common questions

How far is House Leila from the beach and the city center?
House Leila is located just 100 meters from the nearest beach in the peaceful coastal village of Zablaće. Furthermore, the historic Šibenik city center is only a quick 7-kilometer drive away. As a result, this location gives you the perfect balance of a quiet seaside retreat and easy access to city attractions.
How many guests can stay, and is there free parking?
The property comfortably accommodates up to 6 guests. Specifically, the house features 85 square meters of living space spread across two floors, including 3 bedrooms and 2 full bathrooms. Additionally, we provide one secure, free private parking spot directly on the property for all our guests. Moreover, if you have an extra vehicle, there are additional free public parking spots available just 50 meters away.
What makes this the best house for rent Šibenik has to offer for families?
Families love our private outdoor oasis, which includes a fenced Mediterranean garden, a shaded terrace, a stone BBQ, and a refreshing outdoor shower. Most importantly, we never charge hidden “extra guest” fees for families of up to six.
Are there restaurants and attractions near the house?
Yes, Zablaće has local markets and authentic Dalmatian restaurants within a 150-meter walk. Furthermore, you are just a few minutes away from the premium Amadria Park beaches and Aquapark Dalmatia. Consequently, House Leila serves as an incredibly convenient base for your entire holiday.
